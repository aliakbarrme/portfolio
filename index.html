About

Developer first. AI where it pays off.

I run a small WordPress studio and spend most of my time on one question: what can this website do on its own?

Most businesses don't need "an AI". They need a website that answers the same ten questions at 2am, sends the lead to the right inbox and saves somebody an hour a day. That's the work I like.

Restaurants, clinics, a jewellery dealer, a basketball club and an Austrian monastery trust me with their websites. Lately most projects include an AI layer: a chatbot trained on the client's own content, an automation that replies to leads, or an API connection to their CRM.

Process

How a project runs

Clear steps, no surprises. You see working results early and approve before anything goes live.

01

Map the job

A short call about your site, your customers and the task you want off your plate. You get a written plan and a fixed price.

02

Build

I design and build the site or workflow, and train the AI on your own content. You can follow progress on a staging link.

03

Test with real data

I run real customer questions and real form entries through it, then fix what doesn't answer well.

04

Hand over

Go live, plus a short video and notes so your team can update it. Support continues after launch.
